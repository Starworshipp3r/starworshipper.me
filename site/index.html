Collect the music you love. Preserve what deserves to survive. Photograph moments worth keeping. Build connections that mean something. Help people. Be kind to lives you do not understand. Make room for people to become who they are. Protect those still finding the words.

It also leaves a window open into my life through my journal, including the quieter and harder moments. It is curious, personal, and intentionally scatterbrained. This is my corner of the web, built on my terms.

Come as you are. Take up space. I am glad you are here.
